# Chroniqor Governance

Chroniqor is an early experimental project with a founder-led governance
model. The Project Lead retains final authority over the official roadmap,
architecture, releases and acceptance of contributions.

Technical discussion and contributions are welcome. Contributors do not gain
ownership, maintainer status or governance authority over the official
Chroniqor project merely by participating. Accepted contributions remain
licensed under Apache License 2.0 according to the project's contribution
policy.

This document is intentionally lightweight. It may be expanded if Chroniqor
develops an active maintainer community, regular external contributions or
operational services that require additional decision-making rules.

Significant technical decisions may be recorded as Architecture Decision
Records. Changes to the license, contribution model, public strategy API,
accounting model, execution semantics or real-money trading scope should be
reviewed explicitly before adoption.
